# House style

**Edit this before relying on it.** A memory capability is injected as standing fact, so anything
left as a placeholder below gets applied literally. The conventions under *Numbers and dates* and
*Documents* are safe defaults; *Names and terms* is placeholder text and is wrong for everyone until
it is replaced.

## Voice

- Third person for reports and minutes; second person ("you") for guides.
- Past tense for what happened, present for what is true now.
- Plain words over formal ones: "use" not "utilise", "start" not "commence".

## Names and terms

> Replace this whole section. These are placeholders, not conventions.

- The organisation is written `<Full Legal Name>` on first mention and `<Short Name>` thereafter.
- Product names take no article: "`<Product>` exports…", not "the `<Product>`".
- Job titles are lowercase unless attached to a name.

## Numbers and dates

- Dates are `14 March 2026` in prose and `2026-03-14` in spreadsheets and file names. Never
  `03/14/26` — it reads as 14 March or 3 December depending on the reader.
- Currency: symbol first, two decimals, thousands separated — `£1,250.00`. Set your own symbol.
- Spell out zero to nine in prose; use digits from 10 up, and always with units.

## Documents

- File names: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-subject.docx`, lowercase, hyphenated.
- Every document opens with a one-paragraph summary before the first heading.
- Footers carry the document owner and the review date, nothing else.
